Paper 3: Section A Experimental Analysis & Error Propagation

Linearization & Graphing of Orbital Data

A student measures the orbital radius r and the orbital period T of several moons orbiting a distant planet. The raw data is presented below.

Observation Point Orbital Radius r (× 107 m) Orbital Period T (× 104 s) Absolute Uncertainty ΔT (s)
Moon Alpha 2.40 4.20 ± 0.15
Moon Beta 4.80 11.90 ± 0.15
Moon Gamma 7.20 21.80 ± 0.15
Moon Delta 9.60 33.60 ± 0.15

Task Instructions:

1. On a physical sheet of millimeter grid graph paper, linearize the data by plotting T2 on the vertical y-axis against r3 on the horizontal x-axis.

2. Plot your data coordinates, and draw vertical error bars to accurately represent the propagated uncertainty of ΔT2.

3. Use a clear ruler to draw the **Line of Best Fit (LBF)**, and the steepest possible **Worst Acceptable Line (WAL)** through your data bounds.
